The Layer Method™

Diagnosis before product applies to hair, too.

Hair loss can be driven by genetics, hormones, stress, nutrition, medications, inflammation, scalp conditions, or other medical factors. That is why Calibrae does not treat every shedding complaint with the same protocol.

The first question is not “Which treatment do you want?” It is “What pattern are we seeing, what could be driving it, and is this something we should treat here or refer for a deeper medical workup?”

Pattern
Density, recession, part widening, diffuse thinning, and symmetry.
Scalp
Barrier quality, irritation, inflammation, flaking, tenderness, and treatment tolerance.
Driver
History, medications, stressors, hormonal context, nutrition, and medical red flags.

Scalp microneedling

Microneedling is a tool, not the diagnosis.

Scalp microneedling creates controlled micro-injury in targeted areas. In hair restoration planning, it may be used to support the local treatment environment and can be paired with other clinically appropriate therapies.

What matters is whether the underlying follicles are still capable of responding. Microneedling cannot create new follicles where they are no longer viable, and it should not be used to delay evaluation of medically significant hair loss.

Expectations

Hair restoration is measured in months, not days.

Hair cycles move slowly. Early treatment is usually judged by changes in shedding, density, caliber, scalp quality, and photographic comparison over time rather than an immediate visual change after one session.

A treatment series, consistent home therapy, and long-term maintenance may be recommended depending on the diagnosis and response. No ethical hair-restoration plan should promise guaranteed regrowth.

Frequently asked questions

Hair restoration FAQs

Who is a good candidate?

Patients with gradual thinning or reduced density may be candidates depending on the pattern, scalp condition, medical history, and follicle viability.

Can you treat sudden hair loss?

Sudden or unexplained hair loss should be medically evaluated. Calibrae may recommend referral or additional workup before starting aesthetic treatment.

Is scalp microneedling enough by itself?

Sometimes it is used alone, but many patients benefit from a combined plan. The driver of the thinning determines the strategy.

Do you use prescription medication?

Prescription topical therapy may be discussed when appropriate. Some compounded topical medications are used off-label and require individualized medical review.

What is a secretome treatment?

For selected patients, Calibrae may use a patient-specific regenerative secretome as an adjunct to scalp treatment. Expected benefit, limitations, and alternatives are reviewed before treatment.

How soon will I see results?

Hair growth cycles are slow, so meaningful change is typically evaluated over months using consistent photography and clinical follow-up.
